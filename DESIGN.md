# JiKay visual system

Documented from the existing homepage, beta pages and Studio Remote extension.

- White JiKay logo, Helvetica Neue / Helvetica / Arial, and a near-black violet background (`#080611`). White or softly tinted white foregrounds; lavender (`#c9b6ff`) for restrained details.
- Real studio imagery leads. Use full-bleed photography or video with dark overlays for readable foreground content.
- Small uppercase navigation with generous tracking, fine divider lines, and generous responsive page gutters. Body copy uses normal tracking and comfortable line height.
- Marketing headings use large, simple Helvetica with modest negative tracking. Content below immersive openings uses quiet editorial sections rather than repeated cards.
- Buttons and links have clear keyboard focus. Motion is limited to a subtle opening reveal and is removed for reduced-motion preferences.
- Page-specific layouts and behavior belong in `.impeccable/surfaces/`; new pages preserve this identity without changing existing routes.
